# 🗺️ Country Explorer Kids

> *Explore the world, one country at a time!*

A mobile-first geography learning app for children aged 4–6. Explore **144 countries** across 6 continents through fun, emoji-rich flashcards — no installation, no account, works in any browser.

![Age](https://img.shields.io/badge/Age-4--6%20years-brightgreen) ![Countries](https://img.shields.io/badge/Countries-144-blue) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 **144 countries** with fully kid-friendly text and emojis
- 📸 **Wikipedia photos** — real landmark and capital city images loaded live
- 🗺️ **Continent map view** — pick a region to explore
- 🛂 **Passport system** — collect ink stamps as you visit countries
- 💾 **Progress saved** — returns where you left off (saved to browser)
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — designed for phones and tablets
- 🌐 **Single HTML file** — no app store, no install, works offline after first load

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Pick a continent** on the map
2. **Choose a country** from the grid
3. **Tap through 9 flashcard steps:**

| Step | Content |
|------|---------|
| 🏠 Hello | Friendly greeting in the local language |
| 🚩 Flag | Fun fact about the flag |
| 🏙️ Capital | Interesting capital city fact |
| 👥 People | Population and languages |
| 🍜 Food | 4
